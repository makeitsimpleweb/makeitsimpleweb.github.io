Reps Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Overview

Reps Timer ("the App") is a work and rest timer for your workouts, developed by Make It Simple. We are committed to protecting your privacy. In short: the App does not collect or share your personal information.

Your Account

The App has no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Data Collection

  • The App has no ads and no tracking
  • Your work, rest and rep numbers are not sent to us or anyone else

Sharing and Links

When you choose to share the App, rate it, see our other apps, read this policy or send feedback, another app on your device opens, such as your browser, Google Play or your email app. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Feedback

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the App, and keep it only as long as needed for that.

Permissions

The App can keep your screen on during a workout, if you turn that on.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us
