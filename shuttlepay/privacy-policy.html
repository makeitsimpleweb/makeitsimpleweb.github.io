ShuttlePay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

ShuttlePay ("the App") helps you keep track of who played and share the cost with your group, developed by Make It Simple. We are committed to protecting your privacy. In short: what you enter stays on your device.

Your Account

The App has no sign-up screen. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Data Collection

What Stays On Your Device

  • Players - The names you add to your player list
  • Attendance - Who played on which date
  • Bank details (optional) - The bank name, account number and account holder name you enter, so you can send them to your group

This data is stored in a database on your device. We have no servers and never receive it. It is deleted when you remove it in the App, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Sharing

When you tap share, the App prepares a message, which can include your saved bank details, and passes it to your device's share sheet. You choose the app and the people who receive it. Once sent, the message is outside the App's control. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Permissions

The App doesn't request any special permissions.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
